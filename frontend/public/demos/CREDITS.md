# Credits: FormRef demo boards

The three demo boards under this folder open from Home > Demos and Help > Demos:

| Folder | Board | Pictures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `colour/` | First light: a colour board | all 39 below |
| `value/` | Value study: light and shadow | the 8 marked B |
| `poster/` | Red planet: poster project | the 4 marked C |

Each folder holds `board.formref` (the board with its pictures inside, in FormRef's portable format) and `cover.webp`, the card picture, which is one of that board's own pictures with the same bytes: `colour/cover.webp` is The Day the Earth Smiled, `value/cover.webp` is View of the Earth seen by the Apollo 17 crew, `poster/cover.webp` is Looking Up at Mars Rover Curiosity in 'Buckskin' Selfie.

Every picture is NASA material. The files are the same bytes as the copies already credited in two repositories of the same owner, formdeck (`demo/CREDITS.md`, files in `frontend/public/demos/nasa/`) and form.md (`demo/CREDITS.md` and `demos/views/CREDITS.md`); titles, credit lines and source pages are taken from those files. No picture was resized, re-encoded or generated; cropping is done by FormRef on the board and the files stay whole. On the boards each picture carries its credit in its Text field and its source page in its URL field.

Licence: NASA media guidelines, https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/, NASA content "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States" (public domain). The NASA insignia and logotype are not in the public domain and none is shown. The Hubble Ultra Deep Field is public domain as stated on its Wikimedia Commons page ("This file is in the public domain because it was created by NASA and ESA.").
