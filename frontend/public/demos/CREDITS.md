# Credits: FormRef demo boards

The three demo boards under this folder open from Home > Demos and Help > Demos:

Each folder holds `board.formref` (the board with its pictures inside, in FormRef's portable format) and `cover.webp`, the card picture, which is one of that board's own pictures with the same bytes: `colour/cover.webp` is The Day the Earth Smiled, `value/cover.webp` is View of the Earth seen by the Apollo 17 crew, `poster/cover.webp` is Looking Up at Mars Rover Curiosity in 'Buckskin' Selfie.

38 of the 39 pictures are NASA material; the Hubble Ultra Deep Field is published by ESA/Hubble under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 with a credit to NASA, ESA and the HUDF team. The files are the same bytes as the copies already credited in two repositories of the same owner, formdeck (`demo/CREDITS.md`, files in `frontend/public/demos/nasa/`) and form.md (`demo/CREDITS.md` and `demos/views/CREDITS.md`); titles, credit lines and source pages are taken from those files. No picture was resized, re-encoded or generated; cropping is done by FormRef on the board and the files stay whole. On the boards each picture carries its credit in its Text field and its source page in its URL field.

Licence of the NASA pictures: NASA content, not subject to copyright in the United States (NASA media guidelines, https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/: NASA content "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States", and "NASA should be acknowledged as the source of the material"). The credit lines are the ones on each picture's own NASA page, read again on 15 November 2026. The NASA Image and Video Library records (images.nasa.gov) carry no credit line, only the NASA centre, so those rows are credited NASA with the centre in brackets. The NASA insignia and logotype are not in the public domain and none is shown.

Licence of the Hubble Ultra Deep Field: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as ESA/Hubble states for its images at https://esahubble.org/copyright/. This was recorded earlier as public domain on the strength of its Wikimedia Commons page; the original publisher's terms are CC BY 4.0, which requires the credit line to be shown, so that is what is recorded now. NASA also publishes the picture: https://science.nasa.gov/asset/hubble/hubble-ultra-deep-field-image-reveals-galaxies-galore/ (credit there: "NASA, ESA, S. Beckwith (STScI) and the HUDF Team"). Since 0.39.1 the board cards for the Hubble Ultra Deep Field (the picture and its detail crop) carry the full credit and the ESA/Hubble link in their Text and URL fields; a board made from a demo in 0.39.0 keeps the older credit text "NASA and the European Space Agency", marked public domain, and the Commons URL, because a board made from a demo is the person's own copy. The full credit is also recorded here and in `NOTICE.md`.

## The "Quiet structures" starter sample

The sample board that Home offers as the starter template (`frontend/src/ui/sample.ts`) is not a file in this folder. It carries three pictures, `formref-study-01.png`, `formref-study-02.png` and `formref-study-03.png` ("01 / Light & void", "02 / Rhythm & repetition", "03 / A conversation in geometry"). Each is an SVG drawing written in `sample.ts` (flat shapes and two lines of text) and turned into a PNG in the browser when the sample is created. All three are own work, Apache-2.0; nothing in them comes from anyone else. The rest of the sample (notes, a to-do card, rectangles, a connection) is text and shapes made for it, and its link card only points at https://www.archdaily.com/ without using anything from that site.
